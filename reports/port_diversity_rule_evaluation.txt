Behavioral possible-port-scan rule calibration and development checks
====================================================================

Rule:
  unique destination ports per source-minute > 54
  AND source flows per minute > 2,085

Calibration:
  Port-diversity threshold = q99.9 of 176,985 benign source-minute groups.
  Calibration used the six CIC-IDS2017 captures excluding Friday DDoS
  (binary-model calibration capture) and Friday PortScan (evaluation capture).
  The source-flow threshold is the existing 2,085 benign-calibrated threshold.
  Thresholds are not fitted to scan_A.pcap.

Development checks at the frozen thresholds:
  Friday DDoS capture: benign false-positive rate 0.0000%; attack recall 0.0000%.
  Friday PortScan capture: benign false-positive rate 0.0010%; attack recall 97.8777%.

These are flow-level, CIC development-capture measurements. The rule requires
both independent behavior signals; it is a possible-port-scan indicator, not
a confirmed attack-family classification. These CIC captures have informed
project development, so independent validation is still required.
